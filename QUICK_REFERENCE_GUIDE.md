# Twitter Virality Quick Reference Guide

## TL;DR - The Most Important Things

### Top 3 Actions for Immediate Impact
1. ✅ **Reply to EVERY comment** on your tweets (especially in first hour) - Gets +75.0 weight when author engages
2. ✅ **Ask questions** in your tweets - Replies have 27x higher weight than likes (13.5 vs 0.5)
3. ✅ **Get early engagement** - Real-time aggregates update every 30 minutes, so early momentum matters

## What Features Does Twitter Track?

### About YOU (Author)
- ✅ Historical engagement rates (50 days)
- ✅ Real-time performance (30 mins - 3 days)
- ✅ Follower count and quality
- ✅ Verification status
- ✅ Account age
- ✅ Spam/bot flags
- ✅ Negative feedback rates

### About YOUR TWEETS
- ✅ Has image/video (native vs links)
- ✅ Text length
- ✅ Has question
- ✅ Number of hashtags/mentions
- ✅ Tweet type (original, reply, retweet)
- ✅ Current engagement counts
- ✅ Language
- ✅ Links and cards
- ✅ Media quality and type

### About RELATIONSHIPS
- ✅ User-Author past interactions
- ✅ Follower/following status
- ✅ DM history
- ✅ Profile views
- ✅ Time since last interaction
- ✅ Two-hop connections
- ✅ Topic similarity

## Red Flags to Avoid 🚫

### Never Do These
- ❌ Buy followers or engagement
- ❌ Use engagement bait ("RT if...")
- ❌ Post more than 10 tweets/day
- ❌ Use more than 3 hashtags
- ❌ Mention more than 3 users
- ❌ Ignore replies to your tweets
- ❌ Post offensive content
- ❌ Spam or be repetitive
- ❌ Use only external links
- ❌ Post low-quality content

## Emergency Troubleshooting

### Low Engagement?
1. Are you asking questions? (Generate replies)
2. Are you replying to comments? (75x multiplier)
3. Are you posting at right times? (When audience is active)
4. Is your content about YOUR topics? (Topic authority)

### Negative Feedback?
1. Are you posting too often? (Max 10/day)
2. Are you being promotional? (80/20 rule: value/promo)
3. Are you relevant to YOUR audience? (Stay on topic)

### Not Growing?
1. Have you been consistent for 50 days? (Need aggregates)
2. Are you engaging with others? (Build relationships)
3. Do you have topic authority? (Choose 2-3 topics)
4. Is your engagement rate high? (Quality > quantity)

## The Golden Rules

1. **Replies > Likes > Retweets** (in terms of algorithm value)
2. **Reply to YOUR replies** (75x multiplier is game-changing)
3. **Consistency beats virality** (50-day aggregates matter)
4. **Quality > Quantity** (Don't spam)
5. **Build relationships** (User-author scores are powerful)
6. **Ask questions** (Generate conversations)
7. **Use native media** (Images/videos uploaded to Twitter)
8. **Never get reported** (-369x is devastating)
9. **Create value first** (Algorithm rewards meaningful content)
10. **Engage with your niche** (Two-hop effects are real)
